Rosas signed from Contender Series at 17 and has evolved from blitz-submission hunter into a high-volume wrestling grinder. Barcelos arrives at 39 on the best run of his UFC career after decisions over Talbott, Garbrandt, Simon and Jackson.

The hinge: Rosas has never seen round four. Barcelos must survive the younger man’s back-taking pressure, answer in the clinch, and turn the final ten minutes into a veteran’s test.

Guide lean: Rosas by control, with honest caution. Combat Edge’s 66% projection essentially matches the current market, while Barcelos’s +155 still pays for the most important unknown: championship rounds.

Five bouts, all scheduled for three rounds. Current prices are Monday snapshots from BoydBets and Verdict MMA; openers from MMAOddsBreaker.

Növényi brings a 6–1 Bellator run, fluid karate-stance striking and three straight first-round finishes. Haig is back after a 36-second loss on Season 9, carrying a credible BJJ threat and very little margin for another defensive mistake.

The hinge: Haig must get to a takedown before Növényi’s speed and timing expose his overextended striking. The underdog is live early; every clean standing minute favors the favorite.

Guide lean: Növényi by strikes. The price is steep, and Haig’s move from +550 to +354 is worth respecting rather than dismissing.
